IDOR RESPONSE INDICATORS (What to Look For)
================================================================================

200 OK – Success (different data indicates IDOR)
403 Forbidden – Access denied (protected)
404 Not Found – ID doesn't exist
302 Redirect – Redirect to own profile (protected)
Content-Length difference – Different data size
name: "another_user" – Different user name
email: "other@example.com" – Different email
role: "admin" – Different role in response
"data": {...} – Different content structure
Access Denied – Explicit denial
